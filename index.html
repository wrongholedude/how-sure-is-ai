About this experiment

This micro-interaction study compares three ways to communicate uncertainty around the same question: linked sources, qualitative confidence cues, and hedged language.

The goal is appropriate trust—not simply making AI feel more believable. All answers are scripted, and confidence indicators are illustrative rather than measured probabilities.
